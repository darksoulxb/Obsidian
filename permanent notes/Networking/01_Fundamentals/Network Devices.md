# Network Devices

Scrib listed only **Modem, Router, ISP**. Short version for now:

| Device | Job |
| --- | --- |
| **Modem** | Converts the ISP's signal (cable, fiber, DSL) to digital data and back |
| **Router** | Connects your [[Network Types\|LAN]] to the internet, does [[NAT]], hands out private IPs via DHCP, lives at the [[Network layer]] |
| **ISP** | Provides your connection and your public [[IP Address]] |
