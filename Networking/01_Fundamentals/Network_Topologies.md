## [[Bus]]
Everything runs off one single main backbone cable. If that main line snaps, the entire network goes down.
## [[Ring]]
Devices are chained together in a closed circle. Data just loops around from node to node in one direction.
## [[Star]]
Everything connects back to one central hub or switch (like all your devices connecting to your home router).
## [[Tree]]
A combination of star and bus setups. It branches out in a hierarchy—think of multiple star networks hanging off one main bus line.
## [[Mesh]]
Every node connects to every other node. It is highly fault-tolerant but extremely expensive and has scalability issues because the wiring gets ridiculous the more hardware you add.
