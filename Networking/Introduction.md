## what is networking

networking is an aspect of computer science which is used to connect multiple devices through a medium. 

### Identity and Location (MAC & IP)

For data to travel, the network needs to know _who_ is talking and _where_ they are.

- **[[MAC Address]] (Physical):** A unique hardware ID burned into a network chip (like `58:02:05:62:65:58`). It is used to move data locally between devices connected to the same switch or router.
    
- **[[IP Address]] (Logical):** A software-assigned address (like `10.76.120.126`) that dictates how to route data across entirely different networks and the wider internet.
